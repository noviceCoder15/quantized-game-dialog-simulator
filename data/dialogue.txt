Guard: Halt, traveler. State your business in Ironvale.
Hero: I seek the blacksmith. I need a new sword.
Guard: Follow the lantern road past the fountain. Mind the geese.
Innkeeper: Welcome to the Sleeping Dragon! Soup is hot and the beds are warm.
Hero: How much for a room?
Innkeeper: Ten silver a night, breakfast included.
Blacksmith: Careful, the steel is still hot. What can I forge for you?
Hero: A sword that will not break.
Blacksmith: Bring me three ingots of moonsteel and I will make you a blade for the ages.
Merchant: Potions, scrolls, maps to places that may or may not exist!
Hero: Do you buy old relics?
Merchant: Always. Show me what you found and I will name a fair price.
Wizard: The stars are restless tonight. Something stirs beneath the old tower.
Hero: What kind of something?
Wizard: The kind that does not like visitors. Take a torch and a brave friend.
Guard: The northern gate closes at dusk. Do not be late.
Innkeeper: You look tired. Sit by the fire and tell me about the road.
Hero: Wolves followed me for two days.
Innkeeper: Wolves are nothing. Last week a troll asked for a discount.
Blacksmith: I heard the miners found a glowing vein in the deep tunnels.
Hero: Where are the tunnels?
Blacksmith: East of the river. Take the lift, but do not trust the rope.
Merchant: Ten gold for the map, five if you promise to tell no one.
Hero: Five it is. Where does it lead?
Merchant: To a treasure, a trap, or a very large rock. Hard to say.
Wizard: Magic is only patience wearing a pointed hat.
Hero: Can you teach me a spell?
Wizard: Gladly. Start with light. If you can summon a spark, you can summon a plan.
Guard: Quiet night so far. Only a cat and a suspicious barrel.
Hero: A suspicious barrel?
Guard: It moved when I looked away. I am keeping my eye on it.
Innkeeper: The stew today is rabbit, and before you ask, yes, it is fresh.
Blacksmith: Your armor is dented. Hand it over and I will make it sing.
Hero: How long will it take?
Blacksmith: Until sundown. Go have a drink and stay out of trouble.
Merchant: Lantern oil, rope, and a lucky coin. Every adventurer needs the lucky coin.
Hero: Is it really lucky?
Merchant: It has not failed me yet, and I am still here, am I not?
Wizard: The portal opens at midnight. Bring salt, silver, and a steady hand.
Hero: And if the portal fails?
Wizard: Then we will have a very interesting morning.
Guard: Stay on the road, traveler. The forest is not kind after dark.
Innkeeper: Breakfast is served at dawn. The bard will sing if you tip him.
Hero: I will remember that.
Blacksmith: Your sword is ready. It will cut through shadow and stone.
Hero: Thank you. What do I owe you?
Blacksmith: Bring me the moonsteel and we are even.
Merchant: Thieves took my cart last night. If you find it, I will pay a reward.
Hero: Which way did they go?
Merchant: Toward the marsh. Look for wheel tracks and muddy footprints.
Wizard: Beware the mirror in the east hall. It remembers every face it has seen.
Guard: The captain wants to see you at the barracks. Do not keep her waiting.
Hero: Did I do something wrong?
Guard: No. Quite the opposite. She wants to give you a medal.
Innkeeper: A medal! Drinks are on the house tonight, hero.
Hero: I only did what anyone would do.
Wizard: Few would have faced the dragon. Fewer still would have shared the gold.
Blacksmith: A true hero deserves a true blade. Come back when the moon is full.
Merchant: Safe travels, friend. May your pockets be heavy and your pack be light.
Guard: Farewell, hero of Ironvale. The gates are always open for you.
